# Decode latency vs rounds K

Generated by `python -m jevzip.latency` from the latency-*.json files next to it; they hold the exact commands, versions and input SHA-256s. Times are medians of the timed runs. *model*: the decoder's forward calls (step + KV cache, per-round upload of revealed bytes, per-round copy of the probabilities to the host, teacher-forced). *entropy*: quantize + rANS decode on the CPU. *e2e* = model + entropy. K=512 on a lanes model is the AR baseline with a KV cache. *parallel*: all blocks at once without previous-block context (depth K); *codec*: the codec's default layout (codec.default_streams chains, <= 128 blocks a call).

What one round costs on cpu (K=512-style round: 1 new byte + 1 slot per block, cache ~512 deep, median of 48): a bare op + copy to host takes 0.001 ms; a 1-block round takes 7.08 ms with its probability copy and 7.33 ms when rounds are queued without waiting (662 aten ops, ~11 us each). ms per round by blocks per call: 1: 7.08 / 8: 23.39 / 32: 56.87. A round costs ~the same from 1 to 1 blocks: below that it is bound by launching ~all of its kernels and one sync, not by arithmetic; 32 blocks take 8.0x the 1-block round.
